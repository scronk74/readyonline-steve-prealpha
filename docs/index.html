Build digital instincts for life

From a child's first game to a parent's hard conversation to a grandparent's inbox, everyone deserves to feel at ease online. ReadyOnline helps every generation know what to watch for, handle it with simple strategies, and find support. It teaches one habit, the same at every age: Pause · Think · Act.

One habit: Pause · Think · Act

  1. Pause. Stop before you reply, click or pay. "Today" is hurry, and hurry is a sign of pressure.

  2. Think. Who is really asking, and what do they want from you?

  3. Act. Check another way, say no, or tell someone you trust.

Game, text, social media or phone call: same playbook. They rush you, want it kept secret, and ask for something of yours.
