Future potential

Later. These are goals and ideas. They aren't plans with dates.

An app for each age group, tied to the family's phone accounts

The goal is a ReadyOnline app that fits the age of the person using it. Phones already let a family set up an account for each child. Tying the app to those accounts could give each person the right version without asking for a birthday.

How that would work, and what it means for privacy, isn't decided.

A yearly awareness check, with a certificate

Many workplaces run awareness training once a year. The idea is the same for kids and teens: a short refresher each year on that year's situations, with a certificate at the end.

Proposal for Ngoc's review: this idea is new and not yet agreed.

What we know, and what isn't decided

What we know

  • The habit at the centre is Pause · Think · Act.

  • The lessons and the research come from the work of ReadyOnline's founder.

  • This site has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking. Our web host may keep standard visit logs, such as IP addresses.

  • There is no app yet. Everything on this page that looks like an app is a drawing.

  • The Get help page is still a sample. Its phone numbers aren't real yet.

What isn't decided

  • The core message. "Build digital instincts for life, at every age" is a working draft.

  • The age groups, and whether there's a section for under-13s at all.

  • The design, and which version of each page's wording.

  • What the app includes, and when it's built.

  • What a certificate would show: that someone practised, or that they passed a test.

  • How people will hear when the app is ready. There's no sign-up yet.

Who decides

  • ReadyOnline's founder and the person building this site decide together.

  • Two things need outside experts first: a legal check on children's privacy before anything collects information, and an expert review of the help steps before young people rely on them.

  • Before launch, a few people in each age group should try the pages.
