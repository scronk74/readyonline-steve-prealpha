What we're building

ReadyOnline is being built in steps. This page says what's ready, what's next and what isn't decided. We haven't set dates.

Now: this site

An early draft, for review.

  • Pages for each group. Three for young people, one for parents and one for seniors.

  • Get help at the top of every page. That page is still a sample, and its phone numbers aren't real yet.

  • One walk-through you can step through today: a chat during a video game.

  • "Coming soon" on two pages: Research and About.

There is no app yet. This site has no accounts, no sign-up and no tracking. Like any website, our web host keeps standard security logs, such as IP addresses.

Next

  • Drawings of app screens, so each group's app is easy to picture.

  • Simulated app videos. Short clips that show how a practice would play out. They'll be simulations, and labelled that way.

Later

Goals and ideas. They aren't plans with dates.

  • An app for each age group, tied to the accounts a family already sets up on its phones. How that would work, and what it means for privacy, isn't decided.

  • A yearly awareness check, with a certificate. Proposal for Ngoc's review: this idea is new and not yet agreed.
